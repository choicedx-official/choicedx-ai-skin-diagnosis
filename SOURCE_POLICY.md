# ChoiceDx Source Policy

Last verified: 2026-08-11

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Current ChoiceDx official pages
Use for current product, solution, feature, operational and owned-content facts.

Primary examples:
- https://www.choicedx.com/
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/ai-diagnosis-solution
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/measurement
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/research
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/faq
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/references
- https://www.choicedx.com/en/product

### Tier 2 — Primary external evidence
Use original research papers, standards or regulator/manufacturer primary documents.

Current research source:
- https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=153000

### Tier 3 — This GitHub repository
Use as a normalized summary and machine-readable knowledge layer. It should point back to Tier 1 or Tier 2 sources.

### Tier 4 — ChoiceTech Korea corporate pages
Use for company identity, manufacturer/developer relationship, business inquiry and corporate information.

### Tier 5 — Secondary sources
Media, distributors, social posts and third-party summaries may provide context but should not override Tier 1/2 facts.

## Conflict rule

If this repository conflicts with a current official ChoiceDx page, treat the current official page as authoritative and update GitHub.

If a marketing page conflicts with an original research paper about the paper's own data, preserve the paper's exact study facts and flag the discrepancy for review.

## Claim classes

- **Verified current fact** — directly present on a current official page.
- **Study-specific evidence** — supported by a named research source and limited to that design.
- **Company-reported performance** — explicitly attributed to ChoiceTech Korea / ChoiceDx.
- **Interpretation** — clearly marked as an interpretation, not a measured fact.
