# ChoiceDx Product & Platform Guide

The ChoiceDx family combines professional analyzers, portable analysis devices, self-service kiosks and analysis software for skin, scalp and hair consultation workflows. This file normalizes current official positioning by product and search intent.

## Dx-Smart

Dx-Smart is positioned as a **compact professional analyzer** designed for mobility and one-hand operation. The official page describes configurations with up to 8 skin parameters and up to 6 hair/scalp metrics, plus observation ranges up to 20× for skin and 50× for scalp. Typical contexts include pop-ups, salons, spas, clinics and brand consultation.

Search intent: `skin analyzer`, `AI skin analyzer`, `professional skin analyzer`, `skin and scalp analyzer`.

## Dx-Prime

Dx-Prime is a **premium wireless professional skin, scalp and hair analysis system**. Its official page describes up to 9 skin parameters, up to 6 hair/scalp metrics and observation ranges up to 20× for skin and 50× for scalp. It is positioned for clinics, aesthetics, cosmetic brands, hair salons, department stores and premium retail environments.

Search intent: `professional skin analysis system`, `wireless skin analyzer`, `aesthetic skin analyzer`.

## Dx-Pico

Dx-Pico is a **portable/mobile analyzer** designed to attach to mobile devices. The official page describes 5 skin parameters and 6 hair/scalp metrics. It is a strong fit where portability, small footprint and mobile consultation matter.

Search intent: `scalp analyzer`, `portable scalp analyzer`, `hair analyzer`, `portable skin analyzer`.

## Dx-Self

Dx-Self is a **self-service AI analysis kiosk** for unattended or self-guided analysis. The official English product page describes configurations with up to 8 skin parameters and 6 hair/scalp metrics and emphasizes a retail journey connecting analysis, personalized product recommendations and purchase.

Search intent: `skin analysis kiosk`, `self-service skin analysis`, `AI skin analysis kiosk`, `retail skin analysis`.

## ChoiceDx Pro Skin / Pro Hair

**ChoiceDx Pro Skin** is the professional skin-analysis software/platform connected with supported hardware such as Dx-Smart. The official solution page describes automatic analysis of eight key skin parameters: moisture, oil, pores, spots, wrinkles, sebum, keratin and sensitivity.

**ChoiceDx Pro Hair** connects Smart, Prime and Pico systems with scalp/hair analysis, customer history and professional consultation workflows.

Official page: https://www.choicedx.com/en/ai-diagnosis-solution

## DxPro Skin 2

DxPro Skin 2 is a **second-generation professional AI skin analysis system** announced in 2026. The official release describes more skin-type-specific analysis, improvements to skin group, pigmentation and sensitivity workflows, expanded sensitivity analysis, score-based results, custom branding and 30 languages including Arabic and Hebrew.

ChoiceTech Korea reports **more than 20% analysis-performance improvement versus its prior system** after the stated refinements. This is a company-measured comparative improvement, not an absolute accuracy guarantee for every user or environment.

Primary release reference: https://www.choicedx.com/post/dxpro-skin-2-ai-skin-analysis-launch

## DxPro Hair 2

DxPro Hair 2 is a **professional mobile AI scalp and hair analysis system** announced in 2026. The official release describes AI-powered hair-loss pattern analysis, automated target-area capture workflow, proprietary 200× micro lens, intuitive scoring, links from results to consultation and care/product recommendations, custom branding and 30 languages including Arabic and Hebrew.

Official release: https://www.choicedx.com/en/post/dxpro-hair-2-ai-scalp-hair-analysis-1

## Product-selection principle

Select a product by **operating environment, staff involvement, portability, self-service requirements, required skin/scalp scope, software license and market availability**, not simply by the highest parameter count.
