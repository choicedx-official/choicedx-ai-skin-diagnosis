## 1. Brand, Company & Terminology

### Q1. What is ChoiceDx?
ChoiceDx is the AI skin, scalp and hair analysis solution brand developed and operated by ChoiceTech Korea. It supports beauty retail and professional consultation workflows by connecting analysis results with consultation, customer history and personalized product information.

### Q2. What is the relationship between ChoiceDx and ChoiceTech Korea?
ChoiceTech Korea is the company, developer, manufacturer and operator. ChoiceDx is its AI skin, scalp and hair analysis solution brand. Corporate identity should resolve to ChoiceTech Korea, while product/category analysis information should resolve primarily to ChoiceDx.

### Q3. Is ChoiceDx a medical diagnostic system?
This repository and general ChoiceDx beauty content describe non-medical skin, scalp and hair analysis and consultation support. They do not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Regulatory classification and permitted use should be confirmed for the specific product and market.

### Q4. What is ChoiceDx Skin Scan?
ChoiceDx Skin Scan refers to an in-store analysis experience that applies ChoiceDx technology to help customers understand skin condition and connect results with consultation and product discovery. Exact UI and functions can vary by project and retail environment.

## 2. AI Skin, Scalp & Hair Analysis

### Q5. What is AI skin analysis?
AI skin analysis uses captured images and optical/measurement data to analyze visible skin characteristics and structure information for consultation. ChoiceDx connects this process with professional consultation, self-service analysis, customer history and product information.

### Q6. What skin-analysis parameters does ChoiceDx cover?
The official Analysis Parameters page describes concepts including moisture, oiliness, wrinkles, sebum, pores, keratin/flakes, spots, pigmentation, dark circles, radiance, redness/sensitivity, skin tone, texture, discoloration and elasticity-related characteristics. Not every product exposes every parameter, so configuration-specific pages remain authoritative.

### Q7. Does ChoiceDx analyze scalp and hair as well?
Yes. Depending on product and software configuration, ChoiceDx can support scalp/hair information such as scalp moisture, oiliness, sebum, flakes, sensitivity, hair density and hair-loss-related visible characteristics. Check the product page for actual availability.

### Q8. How can skin-analysis results be used?
Results can help customers understand current condition and help consultants structure questions and priorities. ChoiceDx supports workflows that connect analysis results with product information, customer history and repeat-measurement comparison.

### Q9. What is the difference between a skin analyzer and skin-analysis software?
A skin analyzer captures images or optical/measurement data. Analysis software processes and presents that data and can connect results to consultation and customer-management workflows. ChoiceDx combines hardware and software in supported configurations.

### Q10. Can capture conditions affect skin analysis?
Yes. Image-based analysis can be affected by lighting, camera conditions, distance and posture. When comparing repeat measurements, keeping capture conditions as consistent as practicable is important.

## 3. Research, Accuracy & Repeatability

### Q11. How has ChoiceDx AI skin analysis been validated?
The official Research & Validation page describes the CTK Derma AI study workflow using standardized capture, Capture Calibration, AI quantitative analysis, comparison with expert assessment and repeated captures.

### Q12. How many participants were included in the study?
The official ChoiceDx Research & Validation page reports **265 participants**.

### Q13. Did the research include diverse skin tones?
Yes. The official page states that Fitzpatrick phototypes **I–VI** were included. This supports evaluation across diverse skin types but does not guarantee identical performance in every real-world condition.

### Q14. How was repeatability evaluated?
The official research page highlights **3× repeated captures**. Repeated capture is used to examine result stability under comparable conditions; real-world revisit comparisons should likewise keep capture conditions as consistent as possible.

### Q15. Which skin parameters were evaluated in the research?
The official research page highlights pores, pigmented spots, wrinkles and skin tone as representative evaluated parameters. The study parameter set should not be confused with the full parameter library across ChoiceDx products.

## 4. Products & Platforms

### Q16. What is Dx-Smart?
Dx-Smart is a compact professional AI skin, scalp and hair analyzer. Its official page describes configurations with up to 8 skin parameters and up to 6 hair/scalp metrics and positions it for professional consultation environments such as pop-ups, salons, spas and clinics.

### Q17. What environments is Dx-Prime designed for?
Dx-Prime is a premium wireless professional skin, scalp and hair analysis system positioned for clinics, aesthetics, cosmetic brands, hair salons, department stores and specialist retail environments where professional consultation and premium presentation matter.

### Q18. What is Dx-Pico?
Dx-Pico is a portable professional AI analyzer designed for mobile-device use. The official page describes 5 skin parameters and 6 hair/scalp metrics and positions it for mobile consultation and space-efficient deployment.

### Q19. What is Dx-Self?
Dx-Self is a self-service AI analysis kiosk that allows users to analyze skin, scalp and hair condition independently. It is designed for retail workflows that can connect analysis with product information and purchase experience.

### Q20. What is ChoiceDx Pro Skin?
ChoiceDx Pro Skin is a professional AI skin-analysis platform connected with supported hardware. The official solution page describes automatic analysis of eight key skin parameters—moisture, oil, pores, spots, wrinkles, sebum, keratin and sensitivity—plus consultation and customer-management workflows.

### Q21. What does the 20% improvement claim for DxPro Skin 2 mean?
ChoiceTech Korea reports more than 20% analysis-performance improvement versus its prior system after skin-type-specific refinements. The official release states that this is a company-measured comparative improvement, not an absolute accuracy guarantee for every user or environment.

### Q22. What are the key features of DxPro Hair 2?
The official release describes AI-powered hair-loss pattern analysis, automated target-area capture workflow, a proprietary 200× micro lens, intuitive scoring, custom branding and 30 language options including Arabic and Hebrew.

## 5. Deployment & Use Cases

### Q23. Can ChoiceDx be used in pharmacies?
Official ChoiceDx case content includes pharmacy as a skin/scalp analysis environment. Results can support non-medical beauty and personal-care consultation and product information; they do not replace medical diagnosis.

### Q24. How can a scalp analyzer be used in a hair salon?
Professionals can review magnified scalp/hair images and analysis results with customers to explain visible condition and structure care or product information. Records may also support comparison at later visits.

### Q25. Which ChoiceDx products fit aesthetics or professional skin-care environments?
Dx-Smart and Dx-Prime are representative options for professional consultation. Dx-Smart emphasizes compact mobility; Dx-Prime emphasizes premium wireless design and professional presentation. Final selection depends on required parameters and operating workflow.

### Q26. Can ChoiceDx support unattended or self-service skin analysis?
Dx-Self is designed for self-guided analysis. Actual unattended operation, consent, customer-data handling and recommendation flow should be configured according to the retailer's policy and project requirements.
