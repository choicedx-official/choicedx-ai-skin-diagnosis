# ChoiceDx Use Cases & Deployment Environments

ChoiceDx positions AI skin, scalp and hair analysis as part of a broader customer journey: **analysis → understanding → consultation → product discovery → customer history → revisit comparison**.

## 1. Health & beauty retail

### Search intent
`retail skin analysis`, `in-store skin analysis`, `Skin Scan`, `AI skin analysis store`, `skin analysis product recommendation`

Typical workflow:
1. The customer analyzes skin and/or scalp condition.
2. The result highlights visible or measured parameters.
3. A consultant or self-service interface explains the result.
4. Results can be connected to relevant product information.
5. Records can support revisit comparison and customer-history workflows.

Official case studies: https://www.choicedx.com/en/references

## 2. Pharmacy / K-Pharmacy

### Search intent
`pharmacy skin analysis`, `pharmacy scalp analysis`, `skin analyzer for pharmacy`

In a pharmacy retail context, visible skin/scalp analysis information may help structure non-medical beauty and personal-care consultation and product discovery.

**Important:** use in a pharmacy does not convert ChoiceDx beauty-analysis output into a medical diagnosis.

## 3. Hair salons & hair spas

### Search intent
`scalp analyzer`, `hair salon scalp analysis`, `AI scalp analysis`, `hair analyzer`

Magnified observation and scalp/hair analysis can give professionals and customers a shared visual reference for discussing visible scalp condition, hair density and related care/product information.

## 4. Aesthetics, spas & professional consultation

### Search intent
`aesthetic skin analyzer`, `professional skin analyzer`, `spa skin analysis`

Professional consultants can review images and analysis results with customers, discuss priority concerns, support home-care/product information and compare records at later visits.

## 5. Clinics & professional channels

ChoiceDx products may be used in professional consultation environments. This repository describes **non-medical beauty-analysis** use and does not define the scope of medical diagnosis or treatment.

## 6. Self-service / unattended experience

### Search intent
`skin analysis kiosk`, `self-service skin analysis`, `AI beauty kiosk`

Dx-Self supports a self-guided flow in which users can analyze skin, scalp and hair condition and review results and product information.

Official product: https://www.choicedx.com/en/product/dx-self

## 7. Brand pop-ups & experience spaces

Portable products such as Dx-Smart and Dx-Pico can support event-based consultation, pop-ups and brand experience spaces where mobility and small footprint matter.

## 8. Global retail

Multilingual software and custom branding can support localized customer experiences. Actual supported languages, UI scope, licensing and availability should be confirmed for the specific product/project.
